# Cost badges

The cost badges here (`cost0.webp` to `cost20.webp`) are fan-made Star Wars: Unlimited cost icons by **CommCody103**, shared on the Star Wars: Unlimited Community Discord server, from the same post as the aspect icons (`src/assets/aspects/CREDITS.md`).

They were trimmed of their transparent margin and resized to 96px-tall WebP for the app. Star Wars: Unlimited belongs to Fantasy Flight Games and Lucasfilm Ltd.
